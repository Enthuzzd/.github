# Hi there 👋 Welcome to ENTHUZD

ENTHUZD is a technology company exploring the world of mobility, machines, outdoor life, and the communities built around them.

We're a small team, early in the journey, building carefully rather than quickly. Much of what we're working on isn't public yet — and that's intentional. We'd rather show things when they're ready than explain ideas while they're still taking shape.

## How we work

We care about getting the engineering right. That means thinking beyond the first release — solid architecture, clean data models, and systems that can grow without becoming difficult to work with.

We're small, so there aren't many layers between an idea and the person building it. Everyone stays close to the work, and ownership matters here.

## What interests us

- 🌍 **Mobility, Machines & Outdoor Life** — the vehicles, gear, places, and people that make up this world, and how they connect.
- 🛠️ **Engineering & Tools** — software and infrastructure that make complex things easier to build and use.
- 🎮 **Simulations & Interactive Systems** — digital environments for exploring ideas in different ways.
- 🎙️ **Media & Creative Technology** — new ways of documenting and experiencing the physical world.

Not everything we build becomes a product. Some of it stays internal, some becomes an experiment, and some gets discarded. That's part of the process.

## Stack

Kotlin, Java, Spring Boot, PostgreSQL, R2DBC, Hibernate/JPA, Flyway.

We lean toward modular systems, domain-driven design, and technology that solves the problem at hand rather than technology for its own sake.

Most of our work stays private for now. Select projects will go public once they're ready to be useful beyond our own use.

## Where we are

Early, and comfortable with that. There's more being built than what's visible here.
