## Hi there 👋 Welcome to ENTHUZD

We are an engineering team building high-throughput infrastructure and multi-tenant backend systems. 

Right now, we are operating in stealth, laying down our technical foundations, and finding our footing. We don't have any public repositories or codebases ready for sharing just yet, but we plan to open-source some of our core infrastructure blocks as they mature.

---

### Our Technical Stack
* **Languages:** Kotlin, Java
* **Frameworks:** Spring Boot (Reactive & Blocking)
* **Data Layer:** PostgreSQL, R2DBC, Hibernate / JPA, Flyway
* **Architecture:** Modular Monoliths, Domain-Driven Design

---

### Where things stand
There is nothing to install, fork, or look at today. 

If you are a developer working within the JVM ecosystem or interested in multi-tenant data isolation and high-velocity relational database scaling, hit follow. We’ll open up our baseline utility packages once they are battle-tested.
